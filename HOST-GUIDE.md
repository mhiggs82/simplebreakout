# Easy Breakouts: a one-page guide for hosts

Easy Breakouts puts people into the right Zoom breakout rooms in one click. You don't need any training. If you can run a Zoom meeting, you can use it.

## Does everyone need the app?
**No. Only you (the host) need it.** The app moves people into Zoom breakout rooms. They just see Zoom's normal "Join breakout room" message.

Each task and time limit is also **posted in the Zoom chat**, so everyone knows what to do.

## Before your session (once)
1. Add Easy Breakouts to your Zoom (the app owner sends you the link).
2. Use the Zoom **desktop** app, version 5.15.5 or later.
3. Make sure **Breakout rooms** are switched on in your Zoom settings.

## During your session
1. In the meeting, click **Apps → Easy Breakouts**.
2. Under **Quick start**, pick what you want to do:

| Pick this | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Parents like me** | Groups of 4 parents with the same country, city and teen's age. If there's no exact match, people go to the closest group. |
| **Quick chat in pairs** | Everyone talks with one other person for 3 minutes. |
| **Meet new people** | 3 short chats, with a new partner each time. |
| **Mentors with mentees** | Each mentor gets their own room, and mentees are shared out. |
| **Think, pair, share** | Everyone thinks alone, talks in pairs, then shares with the whole group. |

3. Change the question or the minutes if you like.
4. Press the big button (**Start** or **Make rooms**).
5. While rooms are open you can **Pause**, add **+2 min**, go to the **Next round**, **End**, or **Visit a room**.
6. When the rooms close, everyone comes back to the main meeting.

## Choosing who goes to a room
In **Participants**, ticked people go to a room. Unticked people stay in the main meeting (good for speakers, interpreters or helpers).
- **Tick everyone / Untick everyone** does the whole list at once.
- Type in **Search by name** to find people. The buttons then only change the people you see. For example, search "interpreter", then **Untick these**.

## Room size
Press **2**, **3**, **4** or **6** for people per room. Or switch to **Number of rooms** and type how many rooms you want. The line below always tells you what you'll get, e.g. "48 people → 12 rooms of 4".

## Big meetings (100+ people)
- Zoom lets apps make **up to 50 rooms**. With 1,000 people that means rooms of about 20. The app tells you before you start.
- Moving everyone takes a little while. You'll see "Moving people: 340 of 1,000 · about 1 min left".
- People who join late, or who couldn't be moved, show up as **"12 people aren't in a room yet"**. Press **Put them in rooms** and they join the smallest rooms.

## Grouping people by who they are
Some options (Parents like me, Mentors with mentees) need to know something about each person. There are three ways to find out. Use whichever is easiest:

1. **They add it to their Zoom name. No app needed.** Click **Ask everyone in chat to add it to their name**. People rename themselves, for example **"Aisha - Netherlands, Amsterdam, 14–15"** or **"Sam - Mentor"**. You'll see their answers in **Participants**.
2. **They answer in the app.** If people have Easy Breakouts open, click **Send the questions to people with the app**.
3. **You set it for them.** In **Participants**, click the arrow next to a name.

If someone gives no answer, they still get a room. They go wherever there's space.

**How to rename in Zoom:** click **Participants**, hover over your own name, click **More → Rename**.

## If something goes wrong
| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No message in the chat | Open **More options** and check **Post the task in Zoom chat** is on. |
| Someone's answer isn't showing | Check their name has a dash or brackets before the answer, e.g. "Sam - Mentor", and the answer is spelled like the option. |
| Someone wasn't moved | They may have just joined. They'll be included next round, or move them from Zoom's Breakout Rooms button. |
| "Rooms are still open" | Close them in Zoom, then press the button again. |
| You see "Attendee" at the top | You need to be the host or co-host. |
