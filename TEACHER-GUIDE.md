# Global Breakout: a one-page guide for teachers

For teachers and CELTA/DELTA trainers who present in Zoom and then send students into breakout rooms to practise.

## Do my students need the app?
**No.** Only you (the host) need Global Breakout. The app moves students into Zoom breakout rooms, and they simply get Zoom's normal "Join breakout room" prompt.

Each task is also **posted in the Zoom chat**, with the time limit, just before the rooms open. So students without the app still know what to do.

Students who *do* have the app open also see a countdown, a "1 minute left" warning and the task in their side panel. Some formats under **More** (Gallery walk notes, sending insights, the Open Space table picker) need students to have the app.

## Before the lesson (once)
1. Add the app to your Zoom account (your administrator or the app owner sends you the link).
2. Use the Zoom **desktop** app, version 5.15.5 or later.
3. Make sure **Breakout rooms** are switched on in your Zoom settings.

## In the lesson
1. Present as usual. When it's time to practise, stop sharing and click **Apps → Global Breakout**.
2. Under **Quick start**, pick a lesson stage:

| Preset | Use it for | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pair check** | After an exercise | Pairs compare answers for 3 minutes |
| **Role-play pairs** | Functional language, speaking tasks | Pairs choose A/B and act out the situation for 6 minutes |
| **Fluency practice** | Fluency, the 4-3-2 technique | The same task with a new partner each round (3 × 2 min) |
| **Mixed-level groups** | Jigsaw tasks, peer support | Groups of 3 with a mix of levels |
| **Think-Pair-Share** | Discussion, lead-in questions | Think alone → discuss in pairs → share with the class |

3. Change the task text or minutes if you like, then press **Start**.
4. While rooms are open you can **Pause**, add **+2 min**, go to the **Next round**, **End**, or **Visit a room** to monitor.
5. When rooms close, everyone comes back to the main room. Share your slides again for feedback.

**Tip:** **Copy** next to the task copies it, so you can paste it into Zoom's *Breakout Rooms → Broadcast message to all rooms*.

## Mixed-level groups
After you choose **Mixed-level groups**, open **Participants** and set each student's level (or click **Send questions to participants** if students have the app). Then press **Orchestrate**. The app spreads levels across groups.

## If something goes wrong
| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No task in the chat | Check **More options → Post the task in Zoom chat** is on. |
| A student wasn't moved | They may have just joined. They'll be included next round, or move them from Zoom's Breakout Rooms panel. |
| Rooms are still open from before | Close them in Zoom, then press **Start** again. |
| You only see "Attendee" | Make yourself host or co-host. Only hosts can run rooms. |
